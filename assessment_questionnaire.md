# Third-party risk assessment questionnaire (template)

1. Describe services, hosting locations, and subcontractors.
2. Does the vendor store, transmit, or access PHI, PII, or other restricted information?
3. Provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent assurance evidence, with report period and scope.
4. Describe encryption at rest and in transit, and key management.
5. Describe MFA, privileged access controls, and periodic access reviews.
6. Describe vulnerability scanning, patching SLAs, and penetration testing.
7. Describe security incident reporting timelines and escalation contacts.
8. Provide disaster recovery and backup restore test evidence.
9. Identify subprocessor oversight and data deletion/retention procedures.
10. If PHI is involved, confirm whether a signed BAA is required and available.

Record reviewer, date, evidence ID, gaps, risk decision, and follow-up owner. Never publish actual vendor responses or confidential evidence in a public repository.
