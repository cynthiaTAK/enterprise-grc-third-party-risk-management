# Third-party risk workflow and RACI (illustrative)

## Workflow
1. **Intake:** Record business owner, purpose, data sensitivity, PHI access and criticality.
2. **Scope:** Identify required questionnaires, evidence and applicable contractual/privacy reviews.
3. **Assess:** Review evidence and score inherent risk on a documented 5x5 matrix.
4. **Treat:** Record control gaps, remediation owners, due dates, and compensating controls.
5. **Approve:** Route residual risk and any exception for formal authorization by designated risk owner.
6. **Monitor:** Reassess on a risk-based cadence and after material changes or incidents.
7. **Close:** Verify evidence, document reviewer decision and retain per approved policy.

## RACI
| Activity | GRC analyst | Business owner | Security | Privacy/Legal | Risk owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vendor intake | R | A | C | C | I |
| Evidence review | R | C | A | C | I |
| BAA determination | C | I | C | A/R | I |
| Remediation | C | A/R | C | C | I |
| Risk acceptance | C | C | C | C | A/R |

R=Responsible, A=Accountable, C=Consulted, I=Informed. Adjust assignments to actual company policy.

## Escalation example
An overdue critical finding is flagged for security and the business owner; GRC documents the decision and escalates to the designated risk owner. The script does not automatically approve exceptions or calculate residual risk.
